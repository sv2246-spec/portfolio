A little about me

My name is Solomiia Valihunda, and I am currently a third-year Biomedical Engineering Technology student at New Jersey Institute of Technology passionate about medical device design, machining technology and research. I am committed to driving innovation in biomedical technology and accessibility inside and outside the classroom through my roles as the current President of the Biomedical Competition Team at NJIT, a second time member of the Prosthetics Club executive board, and a past undergraduate researcher at the People + Interfaces + Data lab.

In my time off I am an active artist, volunteer, and traveler who loves getting to know people and learning how to do new things.
